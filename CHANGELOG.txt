TINTUITION — CHANGELOG
======================
A colorant inventory tracker for paint departments.
Data is stored locally in each device's browser. Back up from Setup regularly.


v0.96
-----
- Survey "Autofill on hand": a button next to Export that saves the survey's
  counted totals as today's count on the Count & Order screen — so a thorough
  quarterly count also updates on-hand and the order recommendations without
  recounting. Uses the combined total (aged jars included, since they stay in
  usable inventory). Replaces any count already dated today rather than stacking.
- Pour spouts now have an order and incoming display. You can log a spout order
  and see what's coming, even though they're ordered through a separate system.
  Their orders stay OUT of the PO order total (so the terminal cross-check still
  matches) and out of the quarterly survey — they're tracked for visibility only.
- Survey layout: sticks and keys now sit at the bottom, below the tint
  colorants, matching the rest of the app.

v0.95
-----
- Supplies: sticks (1G and 5G), keys, and pour spouts now live in the app,
  counted at the end of the Count & Order screen in whole units. They have no
  usage rate, target, or recommendation — just an accurate count.
  * Sticks and keys are ordered here (1 unit = 1 item, counted in the order
    total) and appear in the quarterly survey as a simple count (no aging).
  * Pour spouts are count-only: not ordered here (you use a different system)
    and not in the survey. They're present so you can check them alongside the
    rest, since the stores store them together.
  Any sticks/keys you were already tracking as colorants are moved over
  automatically, with their count history intact.
- Fixed: "Coming" now shows only what's genuinely still in transit. A delivery
  that already arrived (but you haven't recounted) no longer inflates the
  inbound number — it shows as "arrived, count to confirm" instead. At a glance,
  "Coming" is now an honest picture of what's actually on the way.
- Fixed: a touched order amount could get stuck and hide the real recommendation
  — even across days — so you couldn't get the right number quickly. Now:
  * Saving a count clears the in-progress draft, so each visit starts fresh.
  * Entering a count refreshes that colorant's recommendation, even if the order
    field was bumped — so an accidental tap (pocket, stray finger) is corrected
    the moment you count. Counting is the reset.
  * A deliberate override set after counting still sticks.
  * "Clear all order amounts" now resets every order back to the recommendation.
- The order-total line now reads "X items across Y lines" and includes ordered
  supplies (sticks, keys), matching how the store terminal counts an order.

Known / in progress:
- Usage rate still reads a little high on some fast movers. The correction-
  artifact filter (v0.9) helps, but needs live tuning over the coming weeks.
  Supplies are now out of the usage math entirely, so their wild "needs 40 on
  hand" suggestions are gone.

v0.9
----
- Usage math: correction "artifacts" no longer inflate the recommended order.
  Any single week reading more than 3x a colorant's normal rate (from its own
  calm weeks at that store) is set aside, same as an impossible rise. Self-
  scales per store; real demand surges still count.
- Date fix: evening counts are no longer stamped a day ahead (local date, not
  UTC).
- Running order total by the PO field, updating live.
- Per-order notes: collapsed "+ Add note," saved with the order, shown in
  History, editable. Hidden until added.
- PO field accepts digits only, brings up the number pad on mobile.

v0.8
----
- Fixed: store hand-off files could import blank on a different device. Transfer
  files now identify colorants by name, so a store handed between phones lands
  with all counts and orders intact.

v0.7
----
- SKU and scannable barcode on each colorant's detail view.
- Recalibrated stock status colors (red/yellow/green within a week of target,
  blue for overstock).
- "Arrived" vs "Coming" distinction introduced for unshelved deliveries.
- Single-store transfer (Replace or Merge).

v0.6
----
- Colorant Survey Mode (quarterly count & date, text export).
- Box + jar count entry.
- Colorant detail view with timeline.
- Removed the text paste box for backups.

v0.5
----
- In-transit handling (available the day after arrival).
- Editable per-store averaging window.
- "?" help buttons and first-run explainer.
- Vendor ID moved to Count & Order.
- In-progress counts held as a draft.
